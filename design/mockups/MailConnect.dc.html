Step 1 · first visit

Connect your company mailbox

Sign in once with your Microsoft work account. Your mail stays in Exchange; Sila shows it and sends through it.

Sila will be able to
  • Read and organise your mail (folders, read, flag, archive)
  • Send mail as you, saved in your Sent folder
  • Open attachments, and add them to tickets you choose
It can't read other people's mailboxes. You can disconnect at any time.
